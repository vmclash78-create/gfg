# Example brief

**Brand:** Foodly  
**Product:** Food delivery and local restaurant discovery app  
**Audience:** People ordering meals from local restaurants  
**Personality:** Fresh, fast, friendly, dependable  
**Use cases:** App icon, mobile UI, website, social avatar  
**Avoid:** Generic fork-and-knife icon, chef hat, default location pin, overly detailed mascot  
**Deliverables:** Three distinct concepts first; after approval, primary mark, app icon, monochrome variants, palette, and SVG source if supported

## Expected concept directions

1. **Custom wordmark:** A distinctive typographic move built around the exact spelling “Foodly”.
2. **Abstract symbol:** A compact shape suggesting discovery and delivery without relying on a literal food cliché.
3. **Character or combination mark:** A friendly, ownable device that remains recognizable at app-icon scale.

These are starting directions, not finished designs. Evaluate each against the actual brief, legibility, distinctiveness, and similarity risks.
