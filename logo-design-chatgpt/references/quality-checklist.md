# Logo Quality Checklist

Use this checklist during refinement and before delivery. Mark items as **pass**, **needs work**, or **not applicable**. Do not mark a test as passed unless it was actually performed.

## Strategy
- [ ] The mark reflects the brand's positioning and audience.
- [ ] The core idea can be explained in one sentence.
- [ ] The mark avoids obvious category clichés or gives them a distinctive reason.
- [ ] The identity is not dependent on a temporary trend.
- [ ] Similarity risks to known marks have been considered; this is not legal clearance.

## Form and craft
- [ ] The silhouette is recognizable without color.
- [ ] Negative space reads intentionally.
- [ ] Optical balance has been considered rather than relying only on mathematical centering.
- [ ] Spacing, tangencies, joins, and line weights are consistent.
- [ ] No tiny detail exists without a clear purpose.
- [ ] The wordmark has correct spelling, casing, punctuation, and accents.
- [ ] Letter spacing and symbol-to-type proportions feel deliberate.

## Color and typography
- [ ] A one-color version works.
- [ ] A reversed/light version works on dark backgrounds.
- [ ] Contrast is suitable for the intended background and context.
- [ ] Palette roles are documented, not just listed.
- [ ] Font availability and licensing are understood before commercial delivery.

## Use cases
- [ ] Small-size legibility has been checked at realistic sizes (e.g. 16, 24, 32, 48 px).
- [ ] App-icon crop and silhouette have been considered.
- [ ] Horizontal and stacked lockups remain coherent.
- [ ] The mark works on light and dark surfaces.
- [ ] Print, embroidery, engraving, or signage limitations have been considered where relevant.
- [ ] Minimum size and clear space can be specified.

## SVG and exports
- [ ] SVG has a valid root and viewBox.
- [ ] SVG source contains no unintended external dependency.
- [ ] Paths and shapes are reasonably simple.
- [ ] Transparent background is preserved where required.
- [ ] Actual rendering has been inspected in a browser or renderer.
- [ ] Exported PNG/ICO sizes were generated and checked if included.
- [ ] Files are named clearly and match the documented variants.

## Final review
- [ ] The selected concept answers the brief better than the alternatives.
- [ ] Known limitations and assumptions are disclosed.
- [ ] Deliverables match what the user requested.
- [ ] No test, export, or research is claimed without evidence.
